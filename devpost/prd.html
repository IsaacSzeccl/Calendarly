Calendarly

A standalone, calendar-aware poll for anyone who coordinates a group (a coach, a society organizer, a project lead, someone planning a gathering). An AI reads plain-language constraints and availability and explains the trade-offs.

The story

In a student coach program, the main coach had to arrange a meeting with a group of student coaches. We used Doodle, WhatsApp votes, and Google Calendar scheduling, and people kept asking for more timeslots "because of this, because of that".

The second example is friends and group scheduling (group projects, society members, gatherings), usually 7–8 people. The organizer is anyone who coordinates a group; guests are its members. The pitch and the demo are the same size: a coach and 5 student coaches (6 people, an assumption). Nothing is claimed about larger groups.

Screens: step through each one

"I want to meet with Tom and Jamie this week about our upcoming collaboration. Best to be afternoon meetings. Tom does not like meetings at night. My office hours are 9-5pm. Jamie works in the BST timezone 9-6pm."

The AI turns this into an editable poll: people, afternoon preference, 9–5 office hours, Tom avoiding nights, Jamie's hours in BST. The same box takes constraints from the group ("Tom only does evenings because of work, and the coach can't do Tuesdays"). Initial options come from the organizer's free time.

Demo and success

  • The pitch tells the coaching story at the demo's size; the demo uses 6 accounts (the organizer and 5 guests, an assumption) with calendars designed to produce a "no perfect slot" case. Seeded accounts are labeled honestly.
  • Centerpiece: the AI explains the trade-off and adds slots from a guest's plain-language statement.
  • Length: 1–3 minutes. Scenario: a university group project with 6 students (assumption).
  • Path: describe the meeting (including who is required), share the link yourself, a guest votes, one connects a calendar and sees marks pre-filled, one types a preference and availability and confirms it, the AI adds slots, an earlier voter is emailed, the organizer gets "6 of 6 have filled in", sees conditional advice, picks, and the event and confirmation email go out. Last priority: "meet again".

Now, deferred, and open

What we're building
  • Organizer login, calendar connection, plain-language poll creation.
  • Shareable link and Doodle-style guest form.
  • Guest plain-language availability with confirmation.
  • AI-added slots (max 3) within the organizer's free time.
  • Dashboard: people, votes per slot, AI summary, ranking with reasons.
  • Pick, event creation, confirmation email, "new options added" email, and the organizer's "X of Y have filled in" email.
  • Pre-filled marks for connected guests, guest preferences on the dashboard, conditional advice, and an optional close time.
Deferred
  • Stretch goal (last priority): saved roster and "meet again", with a "remember me" choice for guests.
  • Outlook and Apple calendars; auto-lock voting; monetization; native mobile.
  • Groups of 20 and larger (including 50+); the proof of concept is designed for 4–8 guests (assumption).
  • Invitation emails sent by the app: the organizer shares the link in their own channel.
Open questions
  • Assumptions to confirm: one mark per option, with "prefer not" weighed between "yes" and "no"; the states marked (assumption); the 4–8 guest design target.
  • Group size check: the ranking and dashboard need a check at 20 people before claiming that size.
  • Relative phrases ("next week") and timezone anchoring; the AI-added slot cap; demo calendars; Google verification.
